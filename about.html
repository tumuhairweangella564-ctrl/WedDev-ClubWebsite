About the club

Who we are, why we started, and who runs it.

Why we exist

Web development is the fastest route from knowing nothing to having something real on the internet with your name on it. The club exists so that route is walked with other people rather than alone at 2am with twelve tabs open.

We're beginner-first by design. Every session assumes someone in the room is seeing the material for the first time, because someone always is.
